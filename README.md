# dimghost

Dimghost is a co-op multiplayer investigation/survival game. You have been hired by Ghost INC.
Your job is to go to different locations and identify the ghosts.
You get multiple quests that boost your payment.


But be aware. There are spirits waiting for you at every corner of the map.
